What Subbie is

Subbie is a tracking and reminder tool for subscriptions, free trials and other recurring payments. You enter the information; Subbie organises it, shows it on a calendar, totals it, and reminds you before dates you have set.

What Subbie is not

Subbie is not a bank, a payment service, a financial adviser, or a subscription-cancellation service. It does not connect to your accounts, it does not move money, and it cannot cancel anything on your behalf. Nothing in the app is financial advice.

Accuracy and reliance

Totals, renewal dates and reminders are derived entirely from what you enter. If a price, date or billing cycle is entered incorrectly, the output will be incorrect. Reminders depend on Android's alarm scheduling, which manufacturers can and do delay or suppress through battery optimisation. Do not rely on Subbie as your only safeguard against an unwanted charge. Always confirm against the provider or your statement.
